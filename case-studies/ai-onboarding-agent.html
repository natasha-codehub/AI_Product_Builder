01 · The Surface Ask

Onboarding was a form to decode

Onboarding as a customer of a B2B gas platform normally meant decoding a form: which industry, which bottler affiliation, which cylinder sizes, which service options. This prototype was the AI evolution of a shipped, deterministic onboarding system, the "shipped v1 → intelligent front end" motion, built as proof-of-work for an Avalara AI-Experience PM interview, and as a potential pitch for a client running a $46.4M GMV, 63,000+ customer-location platform.

02 · What Made It Hard

Replacing a form with a conversation, without losing the guardrails

Replace form-decoding with a conversation, but keep the deterministic wizard as the single validated path to submit. The risk in AI-first onboarding is obvious: if the agent can guess wrong on a compliance field or a payment field, the business is exposed. The prototype had to prove the AI layer could help without ever being the thing that actually commits money or compliance decisions.

03 · The Decision

AI where ambiguity lives, determinism where money moves

The wizard is the only thing that can submit. AI guesses are always editable; rule-derived guardrail fields, payment, pickup eligibility, are locked.

Three layers make this real. Conversational intake: chat resolves segment, affiliation, and urgency, then auto-fills the five-step wizard with confidence badges, every field editable or confirmable before a human-gated submit. Document intelligence: upload a prior invoice or license, extract, validate, and route by confidence, auto-accepting the clean majority and sending low-confidence rows to review. Onboarding intelligence: a learning loop from drop-off and exceptions, plus proactive stall detection, setup complete but no first order within five days triggers a diagnosed nudge or human escalation.

Confidence plus a human-confirm gate sits on every AI-prefilled field, the same confidence-gating pattern used in the invoice automation and support agent work. And on ambiguity, the agent asks one targeted question rather than guessing, and doesn't loop.

04 · The Trade-off I Owned

Mocked on purpose

Deterministic, scripted responses make the stage demo reliable, cost nothing, leak no data, and are honestly labeled as such in the UI. The intelligence patterns, confidence gating, exception routing, the learning loop, are the point: they're proven in adjacent shipped products, and this prototype composes them onto onboarding rather than reinventing them from scratch.

05 · The Result

A reusable thesis, not just a demo

North-star metrics defined
Self-service coverage, time-to-first-order by segment, translation-error rate, learning velocity, and guardrail health (the override rate on AI-prefilled fields).

Real domain rules are encoded end to end, segment routing across beverage, food manufacturing, medical/vet/dental, and industrial; will-call pickup cutoffs; card-payment-until-credit-approved, and the wizard submits into the same downstream systems as production: Salesforce, Business Central, Magento. The prototype's real output was a reusable pattern for where AI should sit relative to money and compliance, not just an onboarding demo.
