About

From Production Java to Production AI

Natasha Agarwal
Senior AI Product Manager · Mumbai, remote, US overlap

I started as an engineer at FISERV and TCS, writing production code and building data pipelines. Somewhere along the way I noticed the decisions that made or broke a product were being made two meetings before engineering ever saw a ticket, so I went to where the decisions were. But I never stopped being able to read the code.

Today I'm the sole product manager across two AI verticals at Venture7, a consulting firm with US-based clients. One vertical is a $46.4M GMV B2B eCommerce platform for an industrial gas distributor: Magento portal, Business Central ERP, custom middleware, Salesforce, and EDI automation, run by a 17-person org across five teams that I lead. The other is a behavioral health platform where AI oversight decisions have clinical consequences.

That combination is unusual on purpose. Consulting means I don't get to pick easy problems, and being the only PM means I own everything from discovery to post-launch instrumentation. Nothing gets delegated to a function I don't understand.

Outside work I build my own AI systems, write weekly for a product leader audience on LinkedIn, and read more than is probably healthy. The through-line in all of it: I'd rather test a thing than have an opinion about it.
